## Hi, I'm Fil

> **I started as an engineer. I moved into product because the hardest problems live between the technology and the business.**

I'm a Product and Technology Director with 15+ years across finance, commodities, energy and life sciences. I lead product and platform work, from strategy and stakeholder alignment through to architecture and delivery, and I still build, because it keeps my product decisions grounded in what is actually possible. These days that increasingly means AI, and how enterprises adopt it without amplifying the problems they already have.

## What I do

- **Building product organisations:** product management functions, ways of working and governance, with teams of 70+ across engineering, data, design and product
- **Platform strategy and adoption:** strategy, roadmap and P&L for platform portfolios worth up to $15M, treating platforms as products with users who can choose to leave
- **Cloud, data and AI delivery:** platforms on Azure, AWS and GCP, including regulated environments such as pharma, biotech and finance
- **Cost, security and advisory:** cost governance across clouds, secure-by-design controls and C-suite roadmap workshops
